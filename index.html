About Phoenix Motors

Phoenix Motors is an Indian manufacturer of electric all-terrain vehicles (E-ATVs), focused on building strong, reliable, and mission-ready mobility platforms.

Our flagship model, Sisadrex 1.26, is engineered with a robust and safety-focused chassis capable of withstanding severe operating conditions. The platform is designed for performance, durability, and adaptability across multiple applications.

Phoenix Motors operates on a single validated E-ATV platform that supports modular configurations for multiple high-value markets. This strategy reduces manufacturing CAPEX while significantly expanding the total addressable market (TAM).

  • ⚡ Motorsports & Aesthetic Builds
  • 🛡️ Defense & Security Applications
  • 🏗️ Construction & Industrial Towing
  • 🌾 Agriculture & Rural Mobility

Manufacturing Unit: Chakan, Pune, Maharashtra, India
